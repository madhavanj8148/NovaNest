### KPI-01: Gross Merchandise Value (GMV)
- **Value**: 46590125.0 INR
- **Definition**: Total gross food catalog value of non-cancelled orders.
- **Formula**: `SUM(order_amount) WHERE order_status != 'Cancelled'`
- **SQL Equivalent**:
```sql
SELECT SUM(order_amount) AS gmv FROM orders_table WHERE order_status != 'Cancelled';
```
- **DAX Equivalent**:
```dax
GMV = CALCULATE(SUM(orders[order_amount]), orders[order_status] <> "Cancelled")
```
- **Filters**: order_status != 'Cancelled'
- **Notes**: Verified exactly against delivered order food amounts.

### KPI-02: Net Revenue (Platform Inflow)
- **Value**: 46425992.0 INR
- **Definition**: Total gross customer cash inflow collected on successfully delivered orders.
- **Formula**: `SUM(final_amount) WHERE order_status = 'Delivered'`
- **SQL Equivalent**:
```sql
SELECT SUM(final_amount) AS net_revenue FROM orders_table WHERE order_status = 'Delivered';
```
- **DAX Equivalent**:
```dax
Net Revenue = CALCULATE(SUM(orders[final_amount]), orders[order_status] = "Delivered")
```
- **Filters**: order_status = 'Delivered'
- **Notes**: Includes food price + delivery fee - discounts.

### KPI-03: Average Order Value (Net AOV)
- **Value**: 967.33 INR
- **Definition**: Average customer cash outflow per successfully completed delivery.
- **Formula**: `SUM(final_amount) / COUNT(order_id) WHERE order_status = 'Delivered'`
- **SQL Equivalent**:
```sql
SELECT ROUND(AVG(final_amount), 2) AS net_aov FROM orders_table WHERE order_status = 'Delivered';
```
- **DAX Equivalent**:
```dax
Net AOV = DIVIDE([Net Revenue], [Completed Orders], 0)
```
- **Filters**: order_status = 'Delivered'
- **Notes**: Net AOV = ₹967.33. Gross AOV (GMV / Completed Orders) = ₹970.75.

### KPI-04: Take Rate
- **Value**: 22.48 Percentage
- **Definition**: Effective percentage of GMV retained by the platform as restaurant commissions.
- **Formula**: `(Total Commission Revenue / GMV) * 100`
- **SQL Equivalent**:
```sql
SELECT ROUND(100.0 * SUM(o.order_amount * r.commission_percentage / 100.0) / SUM(o.order_amount), 2) FROM orders_table o JOIN restaurants r ON o.restaurant_id=r.restaurant_id WHERE o.order_status='Delivered';
```
- **DAX Equivalent**:
```dax
Take Rate = DIVIDE([Commission Revenue], [GMV], 0) * 100
```
- **Filters**: order_status = 'Delivered'
- **Notes**: Calculated on delivered GMV and realized restaurant commissions.

### KPI-10: Delivered Orders per Active Rider
- **Value**: 12.49 Deliveries/Rider
- **Definition**: Fleet fulfillment throughput: Total deliveries divided by registered active riders.
- **Formula**: `Total Deliveries / Total Registered Riders`
- **SQL Equivalent**:
```sql
SELECT ROUND(COUNT(d.delivery_id) / COUNT(DISTINCT r.rider_id), 2) FROM riders r LEFT JOIN deliveries d ON r.rider_id=d.rider_id;
```
- **DAX Equivalent**:
```dax
Deliveries Per Rider = DIVIDE(COUNTROWS(deliveries), DISTINCTCOUNT(riders[rider_id]), 0)
```
- **Filters**: None
- **Notes**: Fleet average is 12.49 total deliveries/rider (12.00 completed). Renamed from 'Rider Utilization' because shift hours and idle time capacity are not tracked in fleet table.

### KPI-14: Customer Repeat-Purchase Rate
- **Value**: 99.94 Percentage
- **Definition**: Proportion of registered customers who placed more than one order across the platform lifetime.
- **Formula**: `(Customers with > 1 Order / Total Customers) * 100`
- **SQL Equivalent**:
```sql
WITH o AS (SELECT customer_id, COUNT(*) AS c FROM orders_table GROUP BY customer_id) SELECT ROUND(100.0 * COUNT(CASE WHEN c>1 THEN 1 END)/COUNT(*), 2) FROM o;
```
- **DAX Equivalent**:
```dax
Repeat Purchase Rate = DIVIDE(COUNTROWS(FILTER(VALUES(orders[customer_id]), CALCULATE(COUNT(orders[order_id])) > 1)), DISTINCTCOUNT(orders[customer_id]), 0) * 100
```
- **Filters**: None
- **Notes**: 4,997 customers with repeat orders out of 5,000. Strictly distinct from cohort retention.
