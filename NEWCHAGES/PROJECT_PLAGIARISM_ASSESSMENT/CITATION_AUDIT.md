### 1. OVERVIEW & AUDIT SCOPE

In academic dissertations and technical project evaluations, citation integrity is paramount. Unethical citation practices—such as inventing fake author names, citing non-existent papers generated by LLM hallucinations, or misrepresenting a paper's findings—severely undermine academic credibility.

This audit cross-references every external citation present in the NovaNest master reports against major digital scholarly repositories: **ACM Digital Library**, **IEEE Xplore**, **Google Scholar**, **DBLP**, and the **International Organization for Standardization (ISO)**.
