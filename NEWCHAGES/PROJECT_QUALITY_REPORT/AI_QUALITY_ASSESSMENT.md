#### Epistemic Ground Truth Taxonomy
The system explicitly separates three tiers of information in every response:
1. **Verified Historical Facts (Tier 1)**: Deterministic aggregates derived directly from MySQL or cleaned canonical CSV data (e.g. Total GMV = ₹46,590,125.00). Never modified by LLM inference.
2. **Machine Learning Estimates (Tier 2)**: Probabilistic outputs generated by trained ML models (e.g. Customer Churn Probability = 0.72) accompanied by explicit model confidence and validation status (`PHASE_07_LOW_EMPIRICAL` / `BASELINE_ONLY`).
3. **Prescriptive Simulation Scenarios (Tier 3)**: Hypothetical what-if projections generated by the Decision Simulator (e.g. Projected GMV change of +3.5% if commissions decrease by 2.0%). Marked clearly as simulated projections.
